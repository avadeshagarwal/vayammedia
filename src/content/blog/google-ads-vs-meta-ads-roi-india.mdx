## The Great Platform Debate

Every business owner in India eventually faces this question: should I invest in Google Ads or Meta Ads? The answer is rarely straightforward, and it depends on your industry, audience behaviour, and business goals.

At Vayam Media, we manage millions in ad spend across both platforms. Here is what the data actually tells us.

## Google Ads: Capturing High-Intent Search Traffic

Google Ads excels when your potential customers are actively searching for your product or service. This makes it ideal for:

- **Service businesses** (doctors, lawyers, CA firms) where people search for solutions.
- **E-commerce** with specific product searches.
- **Local businesses** targeting "near me" queries.

### Key Metrics We See

- Average conversion rates of 3-5% for well-optimized campaigns.
- Cost per click ranges from ₹20 to ₹200 depending on industry competitiveness.
- Fastest path to revenue for high-intent keywords.

## Meta Ads: Building Demand and Awareness

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) work differently. Instead of capturing existing demand, they create demand through visual storytelling and precise audience targeting.

Meta excels for:

- **D2C brands** building awareness and desire.
- **Lifestyle products** that benefit from visual content.
- **Retargeting** users who have visited your website.

### Key Metrics We See

- Average ROAS of 3-6x for well-structured campaigns.
- Cost per thousand impressions (CPM) ranges from ₹50 to ₹300.
- Superior for building brand recall and top-of-funnel engagement.

## Our Recommendation

For most Indian businesses, the best strategy is a combination of both platforms. Use Google Ads to capture existing demand and Meta Ads to generate new demand. The exact budget split depends on your specific situation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which platform is cheaper for advertising in India?**

Meta Ads generally has a lower cost per impression, but Google Ads often delivers higher-quality leads because users are actively searching. The "cheaper" platform depends on whether you measure cost per click or cost per conversion.

**Can I run both Google Ads and Meta Ads simultaneously?**

Absolutely. Most of our clients at Vayam Media run campaigns on both platforms. We use Google Ads to capture high-intent searches and Meta Ads for retargeting and awareness, creating a full-funnel strategy.

**How much budget do I need to start advertising in India?**

We recommend a minimum of ₹30,000 per month per platform for meaningful data collection and optimization. However, results improve significantly at ₹50,000 and above.
